# Flare

Flare is a Svelte 5 gallery of scroll chapters with live preview and copyable `.svelte` files. Not a design system, not an npm kit — six pin and scrub gestures you paste into your own project.

Preview. Copy. Chapters.

## Product

- Live preview of each chapter at real height
- One-click copy of the whole `.svelte` file
- Six scroll chapters: Split Masthead, Type Charge, Lane Scrub, Chapter Pin, Mask Reveal, Deck Pin
- Official gsap where the gesture earns it
- Paste into your own SvelteKit 5 + Tailwind v4 app
